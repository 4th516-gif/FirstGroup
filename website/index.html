Protein for Strength Athletes

Most athletes underestimate protein needs during hard training blocks. The research is clear: adaptation stalls when intake drops below the sweet spot, and more isn't always better.

Minimum effective dose 1.6 g/kg
Optimal range (trained) 2.0–2.2 g/kg
Ceiling (diminishing return) 3.1 g/kg
Numbers are teaching anchors, not prescriptions. Your targets are individual.

Carbohydrate Timing & Intensity

Carbs aren't the enemy — mistimed carbs are. Pre-workout loading, intra-workout fueling, and post-workout replenishment each have distinct windows and dose thresholds that matter.

Pre-workout (3–4 hrs out) 3–4 g/kg
Intra-workout (>75 min) 30–60 g/hr
Post-workout (0–2 hrs) 1–1.2 g/kg
Numbers are teaching anchors, not prescriptions. Your targets are individual.
