The work never ends. That is the point.

Stonebound is built for people who train and eat like the outcome is never “done” — only the next honest rep, meal, and night of sleep. Everything here is tied to indexed research or published books: PMID or ISBN, author and journal named. Key terms explain themselves on hover where you see them.

Why Sisyphus

In the myth, Sisyphus is condemned to roll a boulder uphill forever. Albert Camus used the image to talk about absurdity — and about choosing to push anyway, finding meaning in the effort rather than in a final reward.

On this site, the boulder is the endless load of training: volume, intensity, skill, return-to-play. The figure is the person: fuel, recovery, habit, and the “human element” that keeps the work sustainable. Same hill, same physics — different levers.
